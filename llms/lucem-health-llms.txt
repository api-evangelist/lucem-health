# Lucem Health

> Lucem Health is a clinical-AI company founded in 2021 with Mayo Clinic Platform. Its
> Lucem Health Reveal programs apply validated AI models to a health system's existing
> data to surface patients at elevated risk of serious or chronic disease — arrhythmias,
> colorectal cancer, liver disease, lung cancer and Type 1 diabetes — and deliver those
> insights inside existing clinical workflows without changing how clinicians practice.
